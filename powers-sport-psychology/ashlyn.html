About Ashlyn

Ashlyn Hulsey is a Certified Mental Performance Consultant (CMPC), Registered Mental Health Counselor Intern, and holds a PhD in Sport Psychology from Florida State University.

She has extensive experience working with Division I collegiate athletes, teams, and performers through individual consultations, team workshops, and interdisciplinary collaboration with coaches, athletic trainers, strength and conditioning professionals, and academic support staff.

Areas of focus

Confidence Emotional regulation Performance anxiety Identity development Leadership Communication Team dynamics Pressure performance

Approach

Ashlyn's approach integrates evidence-based mental performance strategies with therapeutic principles to help clients build resilience, self-awareness, and sustainable performance. Whether someone is striving for peak performance, navigating a transition, or working through personal challenges, she believes meaningful growth begins with strong relationships and individualized support.

Dual credentialing

Because Ashlyn holds credentials in both mental performance and mental health counseling, she can integrate the two when clinically appropriate. That flexibility matters when performance work touches on deeper themes — identity, transition, grief, or the anxiety and mood shifts that can accompany high-performance life.
