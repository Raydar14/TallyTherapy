Performance vs. Mental Health

Different work. Sometimes the same person

Mental performance is about optimizing what you can do at your best. Mental health counseling addresses concerns that interfere with daily functioning or overall well-being. They overlap, but they aren't the same thing.

Our practice is set up to support both. Ashlyn holds credentials in both mental performance and mental health counseling, so if performance work naturally opens into deeper concerns, she can meet you there. Paige focuses exclusively on mental performance.
