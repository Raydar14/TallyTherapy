FAQ

Common first questions

Q

Do I have to be an athlete?

No. Mental performance work is for anyone whose success depends on performing under pressure — athletes, performers, executives, healthcare, military, students facing high-stakes exams.

Q

How is this different from therapy?

Mental performance focuses on optimizing what you can already do. Therapy addresses concerns interfering with daily life. Read the full comparison →

Q

Do you work with teams?

Yes. Workshops, season-long consultation, observations, and interdisciplinary collaboration with coaches, athletic trainers, and academic support. Team services →

Q

Is this covered by insurance?

Mental performance services are typically not billed through insurance. Payment is made at the time of service. Reach out with questions.

Q

What if I need therapy too?

Ashlyn holds both mental performance and mental health credentials and can integrate the two when clinically appropriate. Our sister practice, Powers of Mind, offers dedicated therapy services.

Q

Which consultant should I book?

Both are trained through Florida State University. See the Services page for how to choose — and text us if you'd like a recommendation.
