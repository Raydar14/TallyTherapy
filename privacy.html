Privacy Policy

How Powers of Mind Psychological Services handles information collected through this website.

Last updated: July 2026

Powers of Mind Psychological Services ("we," "us," or "our") operates the website therapytallahassee.com (the "Site"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through the Site, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to information collected on this website and does not replace the notices you receive as a patient regarding protected health information.

Information we collect

  • Information you provide. When you use a contact form, request an appointment, submit a job application, or email or text us, we collect the information you choose to share, such as your name, email address, phone number, and any details or attachments (for example, a résumé or CV) you include.
  • Automatically collected information. Like most websites, we collect standard technical information such as your device type, browser, pages viewed, and general location, using cookies and similar technologies.

How we use information

  • To respond to your questions, appointment requests, and applications.
  • To operate, maintain, and improve the Site.
  • To understand how visitors use the Site so we can make it better.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

Cookies and analytics

We use Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager to understand how visitors interact with the Site. These tools may set cookies and collect information such as pages visited and time on site. This information is used in aggregate to improve the Site and, where applicable, to measure advertising performance. You can control cookies through your browser settings, and you can opt out of Google Analytics using Google's browser add-on.

Third-party services

Some features rely on trusted third parties, including our online scheduling provider (used when you click "Book a Session") and our form-delivery provider (used when you submit a form). When you use those features, your information is handled according to those providers' privacy policies. We do not sell your personal information.

Protected health information

This website is intended for general information and initial contact. Please do not send sensitive health details through website forms, text, or email. Protected health information you share as part of clinical care is handled in accordance with applicable law, including HIPAA, and is governed by the separate Notice of Privacy Practices you receive as a patient.

Data security

We take reasonable measures to protect information submitted through the Site. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information you have submitted through the Site by contacting us using the details below. You may also opt out of analytics cookies through your browser.

Children's privacy

The Site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through the Site.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
